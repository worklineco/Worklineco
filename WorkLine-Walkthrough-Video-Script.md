# WorkLine Co — Full Walkthrough Video Script & Storyboard

**Purpose:** record a complete demo/training video covering every tab and feature.
**How to use:** screen-record the live site (OBS Studio / Loom / Clipchamp / Xbox Game Bar). Read each **NARRATION** line aloud, or paste it into a text‑to‑speech tool (ElevenLabs, or Clipchamp / CapCut built‑in voice) and lay the audio over the matching **ON SCREEN** actions.
**Estimated length:** ~12–15 minutes at a normal pace.
**Tip:** record at 1080p, hide personal data you don't want shared, and do one tab per take so re‑records are easy.

---

## 0. Intro (15–20 sec)

**ON SCREEN:** WorkLine home / Dashboard, sidebar visible.

**NARRATION:**
"Welcome to WorkLine — our firm's single workspace for running the practice end to end. In this walkthrough I'll take you through every tab on the left, from the daily task register to billing, litigation, and the document tools. Let's start."

---

## 1. Dashboard (45 sec)

**ON SCREEN:** Click **Dashboard**. Show the calendar, the Task chats list, and the Quick notes / scratchpad. Open one Task chat. Add a quick note and colour a line.

**NARRATION:**
"The Dashboard is your morning landing page. The calendar shows upcoming due dates pulled straight from TaskLine. Below it, Task chats work like WhatsApp — every task with a message appears here, unread counts and all, and chats open instantly. On the right is the Quick notes scratchpad: numbered points, a date for each note, and you can colour any line — click into a line and pick a colour to flag priorities. Partners also get two live reports here: the Pendency report and the Open‑task ageing chart, which groups not‑started open tasks by team and by how long they've been waiting."

## 3. TaskLine — the core register (2.5–3 min)

**ON SCREEN:** Click **TaskLine**.

**NARRATION (overview):**
"TaskLine is the heart of WorkLine — the master register of every task. Each row is one matter, with its own Task Code starting with W."

**3a. Views & tabs** — **ON SCREEN:** Click the **Core / Legal / Billing / All** tabs. Open the **views** menu (the bookmark).
"Columns are grouped into tabs — Core, Legal, Billing, and All — so you only see what you need. You can also save your own Views, like a spreadsheet, including filters, sorting and columns, and set one as your default."

**3b. Filtering & columns** — **ON SCREEN:** Type in a column Filter box; open a column filter dropdown; pin a column; resize.
"Every column filters live as you type, and the filter dropdowns only show values that still exist under your other filters, Excel‑style. You can freeze columns, reorder them, and widen or narrow them."

**3c. Add / edit a task** — **ON SCREEN:** Click **Add Task** (or the pencil on a row). Show the grouped form. Point to the red asterisk on Entity Group.
"Adding or editing a task opens a clean, sectioned form. Team auto‑fills from your login, Entity Group is mandatory, and typing a GSTIN or Entity auto‑pulls the group and state from Client Records."

**3d. Reminders** — **ON SCREEN:** Point to the **Reminder Days** column.
"Set Reminder Days on a task and the system emails the people responsible every day across that window right up to the due date — so nothing slips."

**3e. Name/Resource emails** — "When a person is tagged in the Name or Resource column, they're emailed automatically."

**3f. Show Billables** — **ON SCREEN:** Tick the **Show Billables** checkbox; show the reminder popup.
"The Show Billables filter surfaces finished, billable tasks that aren't in the Billing register yet — and a reminder pops up so billing never gets missed."

**3g. Push to Billing & Draft EL** — **ON SCREEN:** Click the green receipt icon in Actions; then open Edit → click **Draft EL**.
"The green button in Actions pushes a task straight into Billing. And inside Edit, **Draft EL** instantly generates the correct engagement letter in Word — the firm's template, filled with the entity, GSTIN and task code, with anything left to complete highlighted in yellow."

**3h. Task Hub & Export** — **ON SCREEN:** Open **Task Hub**; then Actions → **Export view**.
"Task Hub is the chat and mentions centre, and Export view downloads exactly what you're seeing — same filters, same rows."

## 5. Litigation registers — Non‑Litigation, Litigation, GSTAT, CESTAT, High Court (2 min)

**ON SCREEN:** Click through **Non‑Litigation**, **Litigation**, **GSTAT**, **CESTAT**, **High Court** one by one.

**NARRATION:**
"These five registers cover the full litigation chain, each tuned to its forum but working the same way as TaskLine — filter, freeze columns, pin, and save views.
**Non‑Litigation** holds advisory and compliance matters.
**Litigation** is general litigation.
**GSTAT** is the tribunal register — appeal details, benches, hearing dates, and the appeal‑vs‑PH distinction; GSTAT personal‑hearing tasks also flow into the TaskLine overview.
**CESTAT** and **High Court** track those respective forums.
Any GSTAT appeal links cleanly to its TaskLine task by Task Code."

*(For GSTAT, demo: the Actions dropdown, Appeal By column, and column freeze.)*

---

## 6. Billing (2.5 min)

**ON SCREEN:** Click **Billing**.

**NARRATION (overview):**
"Billing is the firm's complete receivables sheet — every bill, its status, and what's been received."

**6a. Colourful summary** — **ON SCREEN:** Expand the **Summary** bar.
"The summary up top is colour‑coded — billing status and receipt status at a glance — and each line is a one‑click filter: click 'Pending' to see only pending receipts."

**6b. Section tabs** — **ON SCREEN:** Click **Client & Matter / Fees & Tax / Invoice & Receipt / All**.
"The long table is split into tabs — Client & Matter, Fees & Tax, Invoice & Receipt, and All — with Actions, Task Code and Client always pinned so you never lose your place."

**6c. Receipt tracking** — **ON SCREEN:** Change a row's Receipt Status; show Amount Received and Pending Amount auto‑calculate.
"Set the receipt status and Amount Received and Pending Amount fill automatically — full amount received when it's realised, full pending when it's pending, and a manual split for part‑received."

**6d. Freeze & fast editing** — **ON SCREEN:** Open **Actions → Column Options**, pin a column. Edit a cell.
"Freeze any columns from Column Options, and edits save instantly — no waiting."

**6e. Push & link** — "Bills arrive here from TaskLine's Push to Billing. If a task was already billed in the old Google Sheet, you link it once by entering its Task Code here."
